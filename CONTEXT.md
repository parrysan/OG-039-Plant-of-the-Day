# Plant of the Day

A daily featured Plant, taken from a Plant list that already exists, shown as one page for the Day. A named Head Gardener is the voice on that page.

## Language

**Plant**:
Any plant — edible, wild, houseplant, ornamental, or otherwise. Garlic, nettles, tomatoes, cacti, and aloe vera all count.
_Avoid_: countryside-only plant, garden-only plant, Polish-only plant

**Plant list**:
The existing set of Plants, written by Phil (with the Head Gardener). A file. Not a feature.
_Avoid_: starter set, suggestion queue, catalog product, nomination pool

**Plant of the Day**:
The single Plant shown for one Day, taken from the Plant list.
_Avoid_: winner, featured listing

**Day**:
A calendar date in Europe/Warsaw, starting at 00:00.
_Avoid_: UTC day, rolling 24-hour window

**Head Gardener**:
The named curator. Thirty years of gardening. Writes or stands behind the note on the page. A person, not an admin role.
_Avoid_: moderator, suggester, editor-in-chief, CMS user

**Note**:
How the plant is *seen* — one short paragraph in the Head Gardener's voice. Sensory, specific, like How Many Plants' "About the plant". Not a care manual.
_Avoid_: blurb, caption, care sheet, troubleshooting, icon-grid facts

**Identity**:
Latin, also-known-as, origin and climate. Lines, not a form.
_Avoid_: tag cloud, difficulty score, "how many green thumbs"

**Cover**:
What you see first. Date, common name, latin, photo. Nothing else. Always elegant, always the same shape.
_Avoid_: facts on the first fold, icons, tags

**Closer**:
The next depth, reached by scrolling. The Note, then Identity, then the Head Gardener's name.
_Avoid_: accordion, "read more" dump, care encyclopedia

**Ledger**:
The last depth on this page. A few labeled lines — water, light, size, toxic or not, one pro tip. Typography, not icons. Troubleshooting does not live here.
_Avoid_: How Many Plants icon grid, Beyond the Basics, symptom/cause/solution
