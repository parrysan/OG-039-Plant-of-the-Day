# Customer language

Seeded. Replace with real notes after the first 10 people see the page.

## Words we want

- “This is today’s plant.”
- “Send this to [name].”
- Common name first. Latin quiet.
- How it looks and feels, then how to live with it.

## Words we do not want

- Green thumbs, plant club, waitlist, recover leads
- “10 tips”, “ultimate guide”, “care sheet”
- Difficulty scores

## Objections we expect

- “Another plant app.” Answer: it is one page a day, not an app store listing.
- “I already have PictureThis.” Answer: this is a gardener’s note, not an ID camera.
- “Who is the Head Gardener?” Answer: a real person, 30 years. Name still TBD.
