## Question

What do existing "plant of the day" / daily plant / plant-a-day products actually do — how they pick the day, what the featured page contains, whether anyone votes or suggests, and which mechanics are worth stealing or avoiding for a localhost v1 with a public Leaderboard and no accounts?

Findings go on branch `research/existing-daily-plant-products`. Point back here; do not paste the whole brief into this ticket.

Context: `research/existing-daily-plant-products.md` on `research/existing-daily-plant-products`.

## Resolution (2026-08-14)

Nobody runs a public *daily* vote. Real products are editorial calendars, date-seeded games, or yearly contests. We are inventing the fourth shape.

Steal the featured page from Wikipedia / Flora / Go Botany (image, common + Latin, short blurb, credit, recently-featured, door to the Leaderboard — no Vote count on that page). Steal cadence from Plantle (one shared plant per Day, countdown to midnight, no account, yesterday in an archive). Steal day-one from garden.org / Tree of the Year (seed 7–14 plants so lock is not empty). House-write the blurb at lock.

Avoid sale clocks, personalized “YOUR plant”, quizzes-as-product, expert-only electorates, login-to-play, and FIFO unused queues instead of Votes.
