## Question

A Suggestion is a Plant proposed for the Leaderboard. What fields are required vs optional in v1?

Common name only? Latin name? Photo? A sentence from the suggester? Anything else? What is the minimum someone types to put garlic, nettles, a cactus, or aloe on the board?

## Resolution (2026-08-14)

Out of scope. There is no public Suggestion. The Plant list row is `date`, `common`, `latin`, `note`, optional `photo`. See `data/plants.json`.
