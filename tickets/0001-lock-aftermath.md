## Question

At 00:00 Europe/Warsaw the top Suggestion becomes Plant of the Day. What happens to that Suggestion, and to every loser, the next morning?

Does the Leaderboard reset to zero Votes? Do losers stay with their counts? Can yesterday's Plant of the Day be suggested again the same Day, the next Day, or never? Is there an archive of past Plants of the Day, or only "today"?

## Resolution (2026-08-14)

Out of scope for this destination. There is no public contest. The Plant list already has the Days. Yesterday is an archive strip on the page, not a leftover race.
