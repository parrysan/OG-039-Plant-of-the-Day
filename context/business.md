# Business

**Product.** Plant of the Day — one plant, one day, written with a named Head Gardener.

**Buyer / reader.** Someone who would send a magazine page, not someone shopping a houseplant database. Garden-curious, design-aware, often a friend of Phil’s or of the Head Gardener.

**Pain.** Plant sites are either ugly encyclopedias or empty pretty pictures. Nothing you’d forward.

**Promise.** Today’s plant looks like Kinfolk and reads like a gardener who has grown it. The link still works tomorrow.

**Current goal.** Localhost page that proves Cover → Closer → Ledger → Practice → Questions. Articles can hang off a plant later.

**Quality bar.** Understand the plant in five seconds from the Cover. One sentence worth pasting. No slop, no thumbs, no SaaS hero.
